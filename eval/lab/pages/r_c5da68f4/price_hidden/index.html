Transparent Engineering

How we made $59 cordless cellular possible

Straightforward answers to the toughest questions about hardware margins, outlets, and pricing.

How can $59 include 3 years of cellular & SMS with no subscription?

We use ultra-low-power LTE-M IoT SIMs that send tiny 1 KB status pings—not video or web data. Wholesale IoT connectivity plus bulk SMS alerts costs under $0.35/month (~$12.60 over 3 years), leaving healthy hardware margin at $59. After year 3, you can renew for $12/year or keep using light-up reminders free forever.

Does it have to stay plugged into a wall outlet?

No. Seniors keep pills on kitchen tables, nightstands, and bathroom counters—not next to outlets. A single 2-hour charge powers the organizer for 30 days cordless, and you get a low-battery text days before it needs the magnetic dock.

Why not a $25 alarm box or $45 Bluetooth organizer?

A $25 alarm box only beeps in an empty room if Mom is napping or outside. A $45 Bluetooth box fails the moment Mom forgets to charge her phone or leaves it in another room. For $14 more, built-in cellular works independently.
