Will the laser lines be visible on brightly lit downtown streets?

Yes—our 532nm green diodes are chosen specifically because the human eye is most sensitive to green wavelengths at night. Under very bright storefronts they appear softer, while on dark side streets, bridges, and rain-slicked avenues they pop sharply.

Does it fit Citi Bike, Lime, or rear-rack commuters?

Yes. The cam clamp opens from 22mm to 35mm to fit seatposts, and also includes a quick rear-clip for pannier racks, saddlebags, or backpack straps.

Can I run the red tail light without the laser lines?

Yes. A single button cycles through: (1) Solid Red + Steady Lane, (2) Pulse Red + Steady Lane, (3) Red Tail Light Only (extends runtime up to 11 hours).
